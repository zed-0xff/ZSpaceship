[h1]SpaceBase Z-001[/h1]

[b]You didn’t sign up for the void — but here you are.[/b]

[i]Spaceship Z‑001[/i] hangs over Kentucky like a forgotten promise: corridors of chrome and shadow, airlocks that hiss, and windows where there should be nothing but black. Suit up. Watch your O₂. Step through light when the ship lets you — and remember the ground is far below.

[h2]What you’re in for[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]The ship[/b] — A full orbital layer over [i]Muldraugh, KY[/i]. Explore, fortify, get lost in the hum of something that was never meant to feel like home.
[*][b]Hard vacuum[/b] — Outside the hull there’s nothing to breathe. Step out unsealed and the void doesn’t negotiate — only rules you learn the hard way.
[*][b]Suits & survival[/b] — Keep the helmet sealed, the tanks honest, and your eyes on the gauges. The UI doesn’t lie; the ship might.
[*][b]Astronaut profession[/b] — Built for this life: pick [b]Astronaut[/b] at character creation if you want a start that matches the ship.
[*][b]Teleporter[/b] — Jump when you’ve earned it. Science matters. Power isn’t free, especially when home is a tin can in the dark.
[*][b]Power & life support[/b] — Flip breakers, manage water, keep the lights from becoming a memory.
[*][b]Your outbreak, your rules[/b] — Tune how many of them wake up in the corridors — sandbox options for ship population.
[/list]
